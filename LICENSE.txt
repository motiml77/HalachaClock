1. HALACHIC DISCLAIMER  -  PLEASE READ THIS FIRST

This program calculates zmanim (halachic times) by astronomical computation.
The times it displays follow the shitot of Luach Ohr HaChaim / Chazon Yosef
(Rav Yitzchak Yosef), and have been checked against that luach's own
published values. They are provided for INFORMATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY.

A calculated time is not a psak halacha. Zmanim depend on your exact
location, your elevation, local custom, and questions of shita on which
poskim differ. Where a matter of halacha turns on a zman - and especially
for Shabbat, Yom Tov, fast days, candle lighting and the end of Shabbat -
you must rely on a printed luach for your locality and on your own Rav,
not on this program.

The author accepts no responsibility for any halachic, financial or other
consequence of relying on the times this program displays.
